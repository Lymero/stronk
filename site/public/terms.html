Stronk is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. The statistics are estimates from past games; they can be wrong, and they never guarantee a result. You decide what to play.

What the app changes in your client

Stronk writes its own rune page and item set, named "Stronk: champion role", and your summoner spell selection, only when you import or when you turn on import at lock-in. It never edits or deletes rune pages or item sets you made, and backs up your item sets before writing. A rune page or item set whose name starts with "Stronk: " is treated as Stronk's.

Your build, set up before the game starts.

Stronk is a free desktop app for League of Legends. In champion select it shows what's played on your champion and role (runes, summoner spells, skill order and items), how much each choice changes your chance to win, and how sure that number is. Your item set goes into the client when you lock in, and the rest is one click away.

Stronk is in development and not available for download yet. The source code is on GitHub.

What it does

  • Follows champion select while you hover and lock in, and shows the build for your champion and role from ranked games on the current patch. You choose whose games: Master+, Diamond+ or Emerald+, right next to the Import button.
  • Shows the whole build at a glance, by name: runes, summoner spells, skill order (your first four levels and what to max), starting items, first back, boots and every item slot. Open any of them to see every option, how often it's played, how many games are behind it, and its win impact against the most played one. The rune page is drawn like in the client.
  • Picks boots for the team you're up against: once three enemies have picked, it goes with what does best against their kind of damage.
  • Goes with its best bet: the option that comes out ahead of the most played one once each number is weighed by how sure it is, so a rarely played option with a lucky number doesn't win. It only says "clearly better" when the difference still holds after correcting for chance across the whole patch, and options with too few games don't get a number at all.
  • Lets you pick anything else, slot by slot, and build your rune page rune by rune. Your picks stay until the next patch.
  • Puts the item set in when you lock in, named "Stronk: champion role" and on top of your shop, with each slot's pick and its alternatives, and keeps it up to date until the game starts (your boots change as the enemies pick). The client can't take a skill order, so it's written on the item set's first block, where you'll see it in the shop.
  • Puts your runes and summoner spells in with one click, or on lock-in too if you turn that on. Flash stays on the key you use.

What it doesn't do

  • It never edits or deletes your own rune pages or item sets. If all your rune pages are in use, it tells you instead of overwriting one. Your item sets are backed up before every write, and you can restore them from the settings.
  • No in-game overlay, and nothing during the game.
  • Nothing about other players: no names, ranks or match histories of teammates or opponents. All it reads about them is which champions they picked.
  • No account, no ads, no telemetry.

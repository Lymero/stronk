Your build, set up before the game starts.

Stronk is a free desktop app for League of Legends. In champion select it shows the runes, summoner spells and items played on your champion and role, how much each one changes the chance of winning, and how sure that number is. One click puts the build into your client.

Stronk is in development and not available for download yet. The source code is on GitHub.

What it does

  • Follows champion select as you hover and lock in, and shows the build for your champion and role from high-elo ranked games on the current patch.
  • Shows the build it will import at a glance, by name: the rune page, the summoner spells and each item slot. Open any slot to see every option with its share of games, the games behind it, and its win impact against the most played option. The rune page is drawn as in the client.
  • Imports its best bet: the option that comes out ahead of the most played one once each number is weighed by how sure it is, so a rarely played option with a lucky number doesn't win. It says "clearly better" only when the difference holds up after correcting for chance across the whole patch; anything else ahead is marked "best bet", and options with too few games get no number at all.
  • Lets you pick any other option for any slot, and build your rune page rune by rune. Your picks are kept until the patch changes.
  • Imports with one click, or on lock-in if you turn that on: a rune page and an item set named "Stronk: champion role", on top of the shop's list with each slot's pick and its alternatives, and your summoner spells, with Flash kept on the key you use.

What it doesn't do

  • It never edits or deletes your own rune pages or item sets. When all your rune pages are in use, it tells you instead of overwriting one. Your item sets are backed up before every import and can be restored from the settings.
  • No in-game overlay and no information during the game.
  • Nothing about other players: no names, ranks or match histories of teammates or opponents.
  • No account, no ads, no telemetry.
